Quick answers

Good questions, clear answers.

How do I start a guided tour?

Type “show me the site” in Nova’s chat. Nova will visit each step and wait for your click.

Why can’t I click the site yet?

Site controls start locked. Type “give me the cursor” in Nova’s chat to unlock them, or “take the cursor back” to lock them again.

Can Nova go to a specific place?

Yes. Try “go to the Aura logo,” “show me the workflow,” or “take me to integrations.”

Are the pricing and integrations live?

No. They are placeholders and concept cards for this prototype.

Does chat need a local AI model?

Guided destinations work without one. Open-ended chat requires Ollama and a local model.
